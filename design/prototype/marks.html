Genie mark, final: Room

A room with an open doorway and lines of text coming in. Single color, Genie teal. Two cuts of the same drawing: the master (64 and 128 px) and a small-size cut for 16 and 32 px with a heavier frame and one thick through-line (the master adds two short text lines), so the doorway slot and the line stay distinct.
